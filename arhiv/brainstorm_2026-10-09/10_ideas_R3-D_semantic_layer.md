# R3-D ideas: the semantic layer of OpenStreetMap as the report alphabet

Brainstormer R3-D, 2026-10-09. Four ideas (D1–D4). None sends a location, a zone, an anchor, a chain or a dwell; the OSM semantics are the carrier: places counted for a rank (D1), a place hierarchy for a relation (D2), street frontage for a speed contrast (D3), place kinds (D4).

**Shared ground (applies to D1–D4).** Host: the P3 public simulator (router, departure profile in UTC+8, segment-time model) with P1 (timed payload), P2 (metrics), P4 (privacy kit), P5, P6 (recovery simulation) and P11 (every train user sends exactly one report, ⊥ in every domain); P10 (stay split) optional. New public input for all four: an OSM semantic export from the same snapshot as the road graph (buildings, land-use polygons, amenity/shop/office/leisure/tourism points, railway entrances; X14), mapped by a frozen tag table and frozen by hash with every design constant before the first u182 run (F8); a one-hour public coverage check (share of nodes with a tagged feature within 100 m, per 3 × 3 zone) precedes the freeze, like P9. Every idea is one item at full ε; users are never split at ε ≤ 2 (X8). Privacy follows the lift lemma X1: whatever the device computes is a path to one value x in a small public domain and only GRR(x) leaves, so P(y | D) ≤ e^ε·P(y | D') for any two users. Sensitive kinds of place (health care, worship, political or sexual-orientation venues, the GDPR Art. 9 categories; cf. the semantic-inference attacks of Wiedemann et al. 2023, arXiv 2310.17643) never form a report category. **Locating power** below = the largest factor by which a truthful report (ε → ∞) can raise the probability that a user's home lies in any one 3 × 3 zone, computed from OSM and the public prior alone.

## D1 Place-rank destinations: the rank law of urban mobility on OSM places, one private exponent
1. **Name and pitch.** Destinations follow the rank-based law of urban mobility (the chance of choosing a destination falls as rank^(−α), where rank = number of places closer to the origin than the destination; Noulas et al. 2012 found α ≈ 0.84 across 34 cities), with OSM buildings and points of interest as the places; each user sends only the decade of that rank for one trip, a number whose law under the model is the same from every origin, which calibrates α.
2. **Report.** One matched train trip drawn uniformly (public law, device randomness); O and D are its first and last node. One forward Dijkstra from O on the public free-flow cost (≈ 40 ms with scipy) gives r = number of places at nodes cheaper to reach than D, plus a uniformly random part of the places at D itself (tie-break by device randomness). Places: every OSM building or tagged point snapped to its nearest node, plus one public pseudo-place per node (N ≈ 10⁵). Sent: the decade of r in {1–9, 10–99, 100–999, 1,000–9,999, ≥ 10,000}, or "loop" (straight-line O–D < 200 m while the route is > 1 km: an unsplit session, F1), or ⊥: GRR over 7 values (under 3 bits); optional half-decades (12 values) at ε 8.
3. **Privacy.** X1, one value, no ε split; frozen: place rule, pseudo-count, cost, decade edges, loop rule. *Home and work:* the rank says how far a destination is in places, not metres, and under the model its law is identical for every origin (all nodes are reachable, so every origin ranks the same N places), so a truthful report carries no information about where the user starts or lives; in the real city it carries only what the model misses (density effects), which a public stress test bounds (the decade histogram per 3 × 3 origin zone under P3's gravity prior gives the locating power; expected ≤ 1.5). At ε 8 a near-truthful report says "this trip passed 1,000–9,999 places", an attribute, never a place. Released: one number (two at ε 8).
4. **Server.** Debias GRR; drop ⊥ and loop (the loop share is reported); gate as F7 (chi-square of the raw histogram against the one the prior predicts through the known randomizer). Maximum likelihood for α from P(decade j | α) = Σ_{r∈j} r^(−α) / Σ_{r≤N} r^(−α), posterior mode under the public prior α ~ N(0.84, 0.2²), snapped to a public grid. Synthesis: departure from the public profile; O from P3's public origin law; draw r ∝ r^(−α̂) and walk O's Dijkstra order until the cumulative place count reaches r, which gives D; route by P3's Boltzmann walk (road-valid by construction); per-segment times by P3. Loops are emitted only as an option.
5. **`sequence_log_prob`.** log P(O) + log P(D | O) + Σ log walk steps + public gap term, with P(D | O) = Σ_{r=R⁻}^{R⁺−1} r^(−α̂) / H_N(α̂), where R⁻ and R⁺ are the cumulative place counts before and after D in O's cost order. They come from one forward Dijkstra per queried origin, cached by (map, place rule, cost) hash and shared by all 17 generators, because α̂ enters only as a scalar in a closed form (≈ 1,100 origins × 40 ms ≈ 45 s per arm, plus P3's reverse Dijkstra per destination). Finite for every sequence (a pseudo-place on every node; P3's floor for unreachable D); members and non-members take one code path.
6. **n = 91.** Under the model the decade shares are geometric, p_j ∝ 10^(j(1−α)): ≈ 0.08 / 0.12 / 0.18 / 0.25 / 0.37 at α = 0.84. Fisher information from the five GRR shares (SD 0.07–0.095 each at ε 2, k = 7) gives SD(α̂) ≈ 0.065, or ≈ 0.08–0.09 with a ⊥-plus-loop share of 0.2–0.3. That pins the median destination rank to within a factor ≈ 2 (trip length to within ≈ ± 45 % in uniform density); a prior error of 0.15 in α (a factor ≈ 5 in median rank) is ≈ 2 SD, so ε 2 moves the one number if the venue-check-in constant does not transfer to GPS sessions on OSM places. ε 0.5: SD 0.44 per share, SD(α̂) ≈ 0.33, the prior arm. ε 8: SD(α̂) ≈ 0.03 and one second number (a rank cut-off, or α at peak vs off-peak). Only at n = 10,000: the non-parametric rank law (12 half-decades by GRR or 20 bins by OLH, ± 0.01), α by origin-density class × period (≈ 9 numbers), kind-specific exponents ("competing opportunities", with D4).
7. **Closest work.** Noulas, Scellato, Lambiotte, Pontil, Mascolo 2012 (PLoS ONE 7(5) e37027; rank law fitted centrally on check-ins, non-private); Stouffer 1940 and Schneider 1959 (intervening opportunities); Simini et al. 2012 (radiation) and Yang et al. 2014 (radiation with selection, arXiv 1407.6256); Lenormand, Bassolas, Ramasco 2016 (J. Transp. Geogr. 51: gravity laws beat opportunity laws on commuting flows); scikit-mobility generators (no DP). DP side: DP-WHERE 2013, Sakong & Zentefis 2024 (gravity fitted on privatized data), Shaham et al. 2022 and Boninsegna & Silvestri 2025 (DP OD tables); a quick search found no opportunity rank under any DP. Versus round 1: C2 inverts gravity from zone and cost-band margins whose law depends on where users start (it needs the origin law and a zone grid); here the statistic's law is origin-free, names no zone and needs neither the origin law nor the trips-per-user law (F2); C1's length scale is a route-choice moment, not a destination law. Versus K1 + K8: K1 ranks a trip's duration among simulated twins (time, a test of the prior); this rank is a closed-form count of real OSM places (space), identifies α at every value (not only under the null) and changes the route likelihood at n = 91 (K1's does not, X11). Versus K2-P (iso-time ring), K5 (modal cell), K6 (EPR, radius of gyration), C4 (anchors): another law, and no place or anchor in the report.
8. **Main risk.** The rank law may fit Beijing GPS sessions worse than P3's gravity (Lenormand 2016; Noulas measured venue-to-venue check-ins), so the prior arm can lose; fallback: the nested hybrid P(D | O) ∝ m(D)·f₀(c(O, D))·r^(−α), equal to P3 at α = 0, at the price of an origin-dependent report law (identification then by public simulation over P3's origin law). OSM mapping density shapes the ranks (uneven building coverage); the origin law stays public, so D1 alone cannot move Geolife's concentration in Haidian.

## D2 Semantic kinship of trip ends: the level at which a trip's two ends meet in a public OSM place hierarchy
1. **Name and pitch.** Each user sends only how closely the two ends of one trip are related in a public OSM hierarchy of enclosures (one campus, gated compound or park) and kinds: inside one enclosure, in two enclosures of the same kind, or across kinds. The relation names no place; the server turns the shares into relation-level constants of the destination law, which produces campus-internal trips and kind-to-kind assortativity that no planned module can produce.
2. **Report.** Public hierarchy: enclosures = closed OSM polygons ≥ 1 ha tagged landuse = residential | commercial | retail | industrial, amenity = university | college | school, leisure = park, or office areas, each with one of 5 frozen kinds (residential compound, education/research campus, work/commercial/industrial, green/leisure, other; "other" never counts as same kind); nodes outside every enclosure are "street". Device: one uniformly drawn matched trip; rel ∈ {internal (O, D and ≥ 80 % of the route length in one enclosure), same kind (two different enclosures of one kind), cross (different kinds or any street end), return loop (O and D in one enclosure but the route leaves it: an unsplit session, F1), ⊥}. GRR over 5 values at ε ≤ 2; at ε 8 rel × kind of D (3 × 5 + loop + ⊥ = 17 values).
3. **Privacy.** X1, one value, no split; polygons, kinds and thresholds frozen. *Home and work:* at ε ≤ 2 neither a place nor a kind is sent; "internal" or "same kind" fits every one of the thousands of enclosures in the 30 × 33 km box, so even a truthful report only says "one of my trips stayed inside one enclosure"; the locating power max_z P(z | rel)/P(z) is computable under P3's prior and expected ≤ 2 because enclosures cover all nine zones. The ε 8 kind of D must pass D4's admissibility rule and excludes sensitive kinds. Released: 2 relation constants (≈ 6 at ε 8).
4. **Server.** Debias, renormalise over eligible users, F7 gate. Destination law = P3's gravity tilted by relation constants: P(D | O) ∝ θ_rel(O,D)·m(D)·f₀(c(O, D)) with θ_cross = 1, so θ = 1 is exactly the prior; θ_internal and θ_same-kind solve two monotone equations that make the shares implied on a public seeded sample of 2,000 origins equal the estimates (the partial normalisers do not depend on θ, so each Newton step is closed form). Synthesis: departure from the public profile; O from P3's origin law; D from the tilted law; P3's Boltzmann walk (internal trips stay on internal service and foot edges where they exist); P3 segment times by road class. Loops only as an option.
5. **`sequence_log_prob`.** log P(O) + log θ_rel(O,D) + log m(D) + log f₀(c(O, D)) − log Σ_r θ_r·Z_r(O) + walk + gap, where Z_r(O) = Σ over nodes v in relation r to O of m(v)·f₀(c(O, v)): three public partial normalisers from one forward Dijkstra per queried origin, cached by map and hierarchy hash and shared by the 17 generators (θ enters as two scalars). Finite: every D has exactly one relation to O; P3's floor.
6. **n = 91.** ε 2: each share ± 0.075 (× 1/(1 − d) for a ⊥ share d). P3's gravity puts ≈ 1–3 % of trips inside one enclosure; if campus-internal sessions are ≥ 0.15–0.2 of matched trips, the internal constant moves by 2–2.5 SD (borderline), and the same-kind share moves only if the prior is ≥ 0.15 off. ε 0.5: nothing (SD ≥ 0.28). ε 8: shares ± 0.03–0.045, so rel × kind of D (≈ 15 cells ± 0.03): kind-specific internal shares and the diagonal of a kind-to-kind matrix. Only at n = 10,000: rel × origin kind × destination kind × period (≈ 50–75 cells by OLH, ± 0.01), a semantic OD by time of day.
7. **Closest work.** Intrazonal trips in four-step models (trips inside one traffic analysis zone get their own share and cost; Ortúzar & Willumsen, Modelling Transport): the same idea on administrative zones, non-private; Cunningham et al. 2021 (POI-derived regions and categories as the alphabet of per-trajectory LDP n-grams; perturbs each trajectory, no relation report, no likelihood); Google Community Mobility Reports (Aktay et al. 2020, place categories under central DP). Versus round 1: D.4/C4 send relations between a user's detected anchors across days (Cond-C4: invisible without linked users); here one trip, no anchor, no linking, visible in unlinked trips; C2 (zone shares), C9 (tokens). Versus round 2: K4/R2-C.2's loop class comes from route geometry and is the estimand there, here loops are a nuisance class that keeps the other shares clean; K5's land-use ablation reports a user's modal class (a habit item). Versus K1 + K8: no twins, no time.
8. **Main risk.** Campus-internal trips must survive map matching (≈ 10 % match rate; whether foot and service edges are in the graph is unchecked, P9); Beijing OSM polygon completeness; "internal" may describe logging habits (users starting and stopping the logger inside campuses) more than travel; at ε 2 one share may stay below 2 SD.

## D3 Frontage clock: what lines the street sets the segment time
1. **Name and pitch.** Each user compares, inside the same trips, how fast they moved along segments lined by shops and markets versus otherwise similar segments without them, and sends one coarse class of that contrast. Both sides of each comparison come from the same trip a few minutes apart, so the person's mode and speed level cancel; the class calibrates how segment times vary along a route with what lines the street, the part of per-segment time (R1) that no planned module learns, with an exact symmetric null for any number of trips.
2. **Report.** Public: per-edge frontage x(e) = share of the 30 m buffer covered by commercial or retail land use or shop/amenity points (high: x ≥ 0.5, low: x ≤ 0.1); public prior time τ₀(e, t) = P3's free-flow time × period factor × HCM-style adjustments for signals (OSM highway = traffic_signals) and access-point density (service-road junctions per km), cited constants. Device, over all its matched trips: residuals ρ(e) = log(observed edge time / τ₀) on edges with ≥ 1 matched point inside (gap-filled edges excluded, X13), outside the first and last 200 m, without a dwell > 60 s (stops are not slowdowns, F1); draw K = 10 pairs with replacement (one high and one low edge of the same road-class group in the same trip, entered ≤ 3 min apart) with device randomness; c = median of ρ_high − ρ_low. Sent: {c < −0.1, |c| ≤ 0.1, c > 0.1, ⊥ (fewer than 3 distinct pairs)} by GRR over 4 at ε ≤ 2; 6 classes (cut-offs ± 0.1 and ± 0.3 nat) at ε 8.
3. **Privacy.** X1, one value, no split; frontage rule, τ₀, K, pairing rule and cut-offs frozen. *Home and work:* the report is a speed difference between two kinds of street frontage, with no place, time or endpoint; the same value can arise on any route in the city (locating power 1 by construction); at ε 8 it reveals a behavioural trait ("shop fronts slow me down"), nothing about where. Released: one number (two at ε 8).
4. **Server.** Debias; pre-registered exact sign test of P(slower) = P(faster): under the null (the two edge kinds share one residual law within a trip and road-class group) every pair difference is symmetric, so the test is exact for every user, mode and trip count. If it rejects, γ̂ by an ordered probit with the public per-pair noise scale derived from P3's jitter, snapped to the public grid {0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5}. Synthesis: P3 demand and router; per-segment time τ(e, t) = τ₀(e, t)·exp(γ̂·x(e))·jitter, so times vary along a route with its frontage; the router's Boltzmann cost uses the expected τ at the departure period, so routes avoid slow shopping streets when γ̂ > 0; P1 stores per-edge entry times.
5. **`sequence_log_prob`.** P3's likelihood with walk costs c_γ(e) = E_t[τ(e, t)] + public turn delay: log P(O, D) + Σ log Boltzmann steps + gap. The cost-to-go per queried destination is cached for each of the 5 grid values (≈ 5 × 1,100 × 40 ms ≈ 220 s, public, built once per map and config, shared by the 17 generators), so the MIA sees γ̂ through route choice. Time stays outside the likelihood (SINTEZA §4.7); finite via P3's floor.
6. **n = 91.** ε 2: shares ± 0.074, the balance P(slower) − P(faster) ± 0.105 (± 0.15 if the ⊥ share is 0.3). With a per-pair median noise of ≈ 0.25 nat, γ = 0.15 nat (16 % slower) gives a balance ≈ 0.42 (≈ 4 SD, detected), γ = 0.05 gives ≈ 0.15 (not shown). ε 0.5: nothing. ε 8: shares ± 0.04, so two numbers: γ at peak and off-peak, or γ for commercial and for school frontage (school hours). Only at n = 10,000: γ by road-class group × period × frontage kind (≈ 12 numbers), a semantic speed field.
7. **Closest work.** Non-private travel-time estimation that uses map context (DeepTTE, WDR, ConSTGAT) and HCM's urban-segment free-flow adjustments; central-DP mean segment speed by sample-and-aggregate (doi 10.1007/978-3-030-78375-4_10), Rameshwar et al. 2024 (user-level central DP bus speeds), Bian et al. 2024 (user-level central DP durations by mode). Versus round 1: C1 sends the user's speed-ratio level (C.1 also speed by road group × period), which at n = 91 measures the walk/vehicle mix (X10, F6); here mode and person level cancel inside each pair. Versus K1 + K8: K1 calibrates whole-trip duration and scales every segment by the same ratio; K8 contrasts two different trips (peak vs off-peak); here the contrast is inside one trip, between kinds of frontage. Versus K3: a speed state per road class at a random second, a level, not a within-trip difference.
8. **Main risk.** Benchmark blindness (lesson 6): no P2 metric reads segment times, so it needs a new metric (segment speed-ratio W1 by frontage class, cheap with P1) or shows only as a small duration W1 change; frontage is confounded with signals, crossings and parking (harmless for synthesis, which needs the pattern, but not causal); thin POI coverage in Beijing; noisy per-edge times; a large ⊥ share if few users have pairs.

## D4 Place-kind mix under a locating-power rule
1. **Name and pitch.** Each user sends the OSM kind of place (residential, campus, work/commercial, rail-station access, green/leisure, other) at one randomly chosen end of one randomly chosen trip; the server re-weights public OSM attraction layers by kind, which moves synthetic trip ends toward the kinds of places the population uses (for Geolife plausibly campuses and stations) at the scale of OSM features rather than 10 km zones, while a public admissibility rule caps what even a truthful answer can reveal about where anyone lives.
2. **Report.** Public kind layers m_k(v) (building and point mass of kind k snapped to node v; class scheme from the published EULUC-China level-1/2 taxonomy, the EULUC map itself not used, S1; rail access = within 150 m of railway = subway_entrance or a station) and soft memberships w_k(v) = m_k(v)/Σ_j m_j(v). Device: one uniformly drawn matched trip, one of its two end nodes by a fair coin, a kind drawn from w(v) (device randomness); GRR over 6 kinds + ⊥ = 7 values at ε ≤ 2; at ε 8 kind × third of the day of departure (18 + ⊥).
3. **Privacy.** X1, one value, no split. *Home and work:* (i) a kind is not a location; (ii) the coin hides whether the kind describes a home, a workplace or neither; (iii) frozen admissibility rule: a taxonomy is allowed only if P(z | k)/P(z) ≤ 3 for every kind k and 3 × 3 zone z (OSM mass only); if the campus kind fails because universities cluster in Haidian, it is merged with work/commercial, so the rule prices utility against home-zone revelation in the open; (iv) sensitive kinds are not kinds; (v) released: 5 numbers (17 at ε 8), none of which can carry one user's home node into the likelihood (unlike K5's ε 8 hub cell, judged a home-cell release).
4. **Server.** Debias, F7 gate; tilt m̃(v) = m(v)·Σ_k θ_k·w_k(v), θ by iterative proportional fitting so that the end-kind shares implied by P3's demand (on a public seeded sample of origins) match the estimates. Synthesis: departure from the public profile (at ε 8 from the kind × period joint, which makes OD time-dependent: campuses and offices in the morning, residential in the evening); O ∝ m̃; D ∝ m̃(D)·f₀(c(O, D)) (P3's gravity, or D1's rank law per kind as competing opportunities); P3 router and segment times.
5. **`sequence_log_prob`.** log(m̃(O)/M̃) + log m̃(D) + log f₀(c(O, D)) − log Σ_k θ_k·Z_k(O) + walk + gap, with Z_k(O) = Σ_v m(v)·w_k(v)·f₀(c(O, v)) from one forward Dijkstra per queried origin (cached by map and taxonomy hash, shared by the 17 generators; θ enters as K scalars); at ε 8 the period is summed out with the public departure profile. Finite: every node keeps a public pseudo-mass.
6. **n = 91.** ε 2: kind shares ± 0.077 (× 1/(1 − d)); a campus share of 0.30 against a prior of 0.08 is ≈ 2.9 SD (survives), a station share of 0.15 against 0.02 is ≈ 1.7 SD (borderline). ε 0.5: nothing. ε 8: 18 kind × period cells ± 0.03, a time-dependent kind mix. Only at n = 10,000: 12 level-2 kinds × 4 periods (48 cells by OLH, ± 0.012) and kind-specific decay or rank exponents.
7. **Closest work.** Google Community Mobility Reports (Aktay et al. 2020, arXiv 2004.04145: visits by place category under central DP with per-user bounds; no LDP, no generator); Cunningham et al. 2021 (POI-category hierarchy as the per-trajectory LDP alphabet); DP-WHERE 2013; Kapp et al. 2022 (revealing which categories appear can expose visits). Versus round 1: C2's 3 × 3 zone shares (the head-to-head: cell JSD and 3 × 3 OD JSD on the same users), C3's mixture weights over public behaviour generators (here the components are public spatial layers), C4's anchor types. Versus round 2: K5's land-use ablation reports a user's modal class (a habit and home proxy), D4 a random end; K2-W infers purpose from dwell, D4 uses no dwell, purpose or chain; nothing shared with K1 + K8 beyond GRR.
8. **Main risk.** Novelty: close to "C2 with a semantic alphabet", and a new alphabet alone is a close variant (lesson 1); its own claims (the admissibility rule, feature-scale relocation, time-dependent OD at ε 8) are modest. OSM land-use coverage in Beijing; the rule may forbid the most useful kind.

## Self-ranking
1. D1 Place-rank destinations: the strongest structural claim (a report law that is the same from every origin, so one number is identified without the origin law, the trips-per-user law or any location), a closed-form likelihood the MIA can see, a cited prior constant; risk: how well the rank law fits Beijing sessions.
2. D3 Frontage clock: the most novel estimand (within-trip, mode-cancelling, exact null for any trip count) and the only planned private signal on within-route segment times beyond road class (R1), but invisible on today's benchmark without a new metric.
3. D2 Semantic kinship: a new non-locating relation with an exact relation-tilted likelihood, hinging on campus-internal trips surviving map matching; D4 is the lens-obvious fallback and the natural head-to-head with C2.
